About me

I’m a Product Designer from Brazil who loves turning complex problems into simple, beautiful, and intuitive experiences. But what really sets my work apart is how I design.

I’ve built an AI-integrated workflow that combines Claude MCP connected directly to Figma, Figma Make, and Claude AI to accelerate every stage of the design process — from building scalable Design Systems to delivering production-ready handoffs with component-by-component HTML/CSS/JS code. The result? Faster iterations, fewer back-and-forths with devs, and more time focused on what actually matters: the user experience.

My process is grounded in UX Strategy and Design Thinking — I conduct structured research (interviews, shadowing, usability testing), map user journeys, and use data to make confident product decisions. I don’t just make things look good; I make sure they solve the right problem in the right way.

I also bring a solid foundation in HTML, CSS, and JS, which means I speak the dev team’s language and design with implementation in mind from day one.

Whether it’s a fintech app, an enterprise platform, or a bold e-commerce experience — I bring craft, strategy, and a genuine obsession with quality to everything I build.

Pockit
By Pedro Zorzenon
UX Research UI Design Fintech Mobile App Design System
PROBLEM

Many people face difficulties in investing and saving money due to the complexity of existing financial apps. Financial management options are often not intuitive and are not tailored to different age groups or levels of financial knowledge.

  • Difficulties in investing and saving
  • Complexity of financial apps
  • Lack of Incentives
  • Lack of Financial Overview
  • Digital Security concerns
SOLUTION

Pockit enables and encourages users to save and invest their money mindfully and easily. The main goal is to provide a simple and intuitive experience for people of all ages, democratizing access to financial tools in an inclusive way.

  • Simple and Personalized Tutorials
  • Gamification: Incentivizing financial goals
  • Focus on Security
  • Comprehensive Dashboard
  • Financial Education

DISCOVER

Desk Research

During the development of Pockit, several issues faced by users in managing their finances were identified:

1 — Low Financial Literacy

Many people do not know how or where to start investing. According to the OECD, only 26% of Brazilians have basic knowledge of finance. This highlights the need for tools that educate users while providing practical solutions.

Source: OECD — Financial Literacy Report

2 — Lack of Personalization

Many apps do not allow users to adjust the system to their specific goals. According to McKinsey, 71% of consumers expect personalized interactions in their digital experiences, but many financial apps fail to deliver this.

Source: McKinsey — The value of getting personalization right

3 — Complex Interfaces

An overload of information or steps confuses new users. A study by the Nielsen Norman Group indicates that 58% of users abandon apps because they find them complicated to navigate.

Source: NNGroup — Mobile Usability Report

Benchmarks

Benchmark analysis was essential in identifying gaps in the market, understanding what already works, and avoiding common mistakes made by other financial apps.

User Research

To better understand the needs and difficulties of users, the following questions were created for in-depth interviews:

1
What are the biggest challenges you face when trying to save or invest money?
2
Have you ever used a financial app? What did you think of the experience?
3
What features would you like to see in a financial management app?
4
What motivates you to save or invest?
5
Is there any feature that you find confusing or complicated in current financial apps?
6
What is your biggest concern when using technology to manage your finances?
7
Do you prefer a more minimalist or detailed design in financial apps? Why?

Usability Tests

Real-life scenario tests were planned to evaluate the effectiveness of the design and user understanding.

1 — Registration and Box Setup: Assess whether users can create boxes for their goals without difficulties.

3 — Goal Tracking: Check if the graphs and indicators help users monitor their progress.
4 — Portfolio Exploration: Explore the "Portfolio" screen to understand total assets divided among created boxes and applied investments.

HourGlass
HourGlass
By Pedro Zorzenon
UX Research UI Design B2B Mobile App SaaS
PROBLEM

Companies face many challenges when managing schedules, overtime, responsibilities and communication between employees. The lack of a centralized tool leads to confusion and loss of productivity.

  • Lack of clarity in communication
  • Difficulty managing schedules
  • Inefficient management of incidents
  • Difficulty tracking multiple tasks
  • Disorganization within teams
SOLUTION

HourGlass is an application designed to organize companies, centralizing responsibilities, events, observations and employee management to optimize internal processes.

  • Efficient management of multiple employees
  • Automated time tracking and records
  • Smart management of events and notes
  • Notifications and alerts for teams
  • Simplified and accessible reports
Export Figma hero screens → components/images/hourglass/screens.png

DISCOVER

Data Search

Goal:

Understand the needs and challenges faced by companies in relation to time management and internal communication.

Methodologies
  • Interviews with Managers and Employees: Qualitative interviews to understand real operational problems and needs.
  • Questionnaires: Distribution of quantitative questionnaires to collect data on the frequency and impact of the identified problems.
  • Benchmarking: Study of other solutions available on the market to identify strengths and weaknesses.
Results
  • Main Pain Points: Lack of clarity in communication, difficulty managing schedules and overtime, and confusion about observations and complaints.
  • Priority Needs: A centralized, easy-to-use tool that allows clear communication, accurate records and management of schedules and roles.

DEFINE

CARLA MENDES

Age34

Background

Carla manages a team of 20+ employees across multiple shifts. She struggles with spreadsheets and messaging apps to track attendance, overtime and internal complaints — constantly losing information and wasting time.

Goals and Frustrations

She wants a single platform where she can visualize schedules, manage complaints and track employee performance without switching between tools.

Pains and Desires

She desires clear automated notifications and a dashboard that gives her a complete overview of her team in real time.

Key Insights
  • Managers lose 2+ hours/day managing information across disconnected tools.
  • Employees miss important updates due to lack of centralized notifications.
  • Overtime tracking is done manually, causing disputes and errors.
  • Internal complaints lack a formal channel, creating friction and distrust.
